01 / About

A little about me.

I'm a 3D designer and Roblox developer who loves the low poly style: clean shapes, bold colours and assets that read well at any distance and run smoothly on every device.

I've been working in Roblox for 7 months and I'm currently building two games of my own. That means I do the whole pipeline myself: modelling in Blender, building the map, and scripting the systems that make everything work.

  • 2games in progress
